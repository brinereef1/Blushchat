Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information BlushedChat ("we", "us", "our") collects and how it is used when you visit and use our random chat website. We have designed BlushedChat to be privacy-first: because there are no accounts, we simply do not collect the personal data that most chat apps collect.

1. Information We Collect

BlushedChat does not require an account, an email address, a phone number, or a password. You may enter an optional nickname, age, and gender when you start a chat. This information is used only to display your profile to the stranger you are matched with and is not stored on our servers in a way that identifies you.

  • Optional nickname — used only to show your display name.
  • Age and gender — used only for matching and filters.
  • Technical data — our server logs may record basic connection details such as IP address and connection timestamps for security and abuse prevention. These logs are not linked to any account.

2. Local Storage

We use your browser's local storage to remember lightweight preferences, such as your chosen theme (dark or light). This data stays on your device and is never transmitted to us.

3. Cookies and Third-Party Services

BlushedChat itself sets no tracking cookies. If advertising is enabled on the site, third-party advertising providers (such as Google AdSense) may use cookies to serve ads. These providers have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review them for details on how they handle data.

4. Chat Content and Files

Chat messages and shared files are delivered between users in real time. We do not store chat history or uploaded files on our servers after a chat session ends. Never share personal, financial, or sensitive information with strangers, and be aware that anything you share during a chat can be seen by the person you are talking to.

5. Children's Privacy

BlushedChat is intended for adults aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has used the service, please contact us so we can investigate.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information processed by our service, including encryption in transit. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your data, such as access, correction, or deletion. Because we store minimal data and no accounts, these requests can generally be fulfilled simply by stopping your use of the service. For anything else, contact us using the details below.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Your continued use of BlushedChat after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at our contact page or email santra.brine2020@gmail.com.
