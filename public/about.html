About BlushedChat

A free random chat website for anonymous conversations with strangers.

BlushedChat is a free random chat website that connects you with strangers from around the world in seconds. We built BlushedChat around one simple idea: meeting new people should be instant, private, and completely free. There is no account to create, no app to download, and no personal information to hand over. Open the site in any browser, choose an optional name, and start chatting with a random stranger right away.

Why We Made BlushedChat

Most anonymous chat apps ask for an email, a phone number, and a password before you can talk to anyone. We believe the best way to chat with strangers anonymously is to remove those barriers completely. BlushedChat runs entirely in your web browser on any device, so you can enjoy free random chat wherever you are — on desktop, tablet, or phone.

How It Works

Starting a random chat is simple. Enter an optional nickname, pick your age and gender, and click Find a Connection. The app instantly matches you with a random stranger for a private one-on-one text chat. You can type messages, share photos, videos, and audio files, or press Skip to meet the next person. When you are done, just leave — you disappear instantly and anonymously.

Features You'll Love

  • Instant matching with a random chat with stranger connection
  • Optional gender and age filters for chat with strangers online
  • Real-time text chat with typing indicators
  • Share images, videos, and audio files
  • Skip to a new partner with one tap
  • Dark and light themes
  • No sign up, no downloads, no fees

Privacy First

Because BlushedChat has no accounts, we never store your name, email, or phone number. Your privacy is protected by design: every conversation is ephemeral, and leaving a chat removes you instantly. We do recommend basic common sense — never share personal information such as your full name, address, or banking details with a stranger. BlushedChat is intended for adults aged 18 and older.
